Verdict: NOT-READY

Third review. Of the 13 findings in `spec-review-2.md`, S1–S9 and S11–S13 are fixed. S1 is fixed by giving the late loop its own limit. S2 is fixed by the "Stage result" section. S3 is fixed by Q41 and the "Spec changes during test-case review" section.
