---
title: Answering questions
description: How to get the most out of the clarifying questions.
---

The answers are the only place where the product's real rules enter the change. A few habits make them count.

## Forward, don't guess

Every question makes sense on its own and says why it is asked. When you do not know the answer, send the
question as it is to the person who does, and bring the reply back. A guessed answer looks the same as a real
one in every later artifact.

## Treat a recommendation as a suggestion

A question may carry `Recommended: B` with a reason, when a source, a spec or the code supports an option. It is
never an answer: each question needs its own, and "accept all your recommendations" does not count.

## Say "Not yet defined" when it is true

It is a valid answer. It becomes an open question or an explicit assumption in the proposal, where everyone can
see it. The alternative is detail the agent invents.

## Be specific

The agent checks answers for vague words and contradictions and asks follow-ups. "Fast", "as usual" and "up to
you" all come back as a new question, so answer with the number, the rule or the example straight away.

## Read the digest

After the specs the agent shows a digest of at most 15 lines: what will be built, what is left out and what it
decided itself. It does not wait for you. Say "stop" if the scope is wrong; that is the cheapest moment to
correct it.
