## What to notice

- **You act at three stages**: the brief, the answers and the archive. The rest runs without you.
- **The questions are the point.** "Can a code be combined with a sale price?" is exactly the kind of gap a
  one-line brief leaves, and the kind of bug manual QA would file later.
- **Each stage leaves a file.** The answers feed the proposal, the proposal feeds the specs, a scenario gets a
  level, and a test carries the scenario's marker. Nothing is passed along in chat only.
- **Backend and frontend share the specs.** Once the scenarios exist, neither side waits for the other to
  explain what the feature does.

## Practice: forward a question, don't guess it

Every question is written so you can paste it to whoever knows the answer. If the product owner decides how
discounts stack, send them Q3 as it is and bring the reply back into `clarifications.md`.
